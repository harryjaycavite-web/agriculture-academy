General Economics

📘 Scarcity & Choice

  • Scarcity — unlimited wants vs. limited resources force every economic decision.
  • Opportunity Cost — the value of the next-best alternative given up when a choice is made.
  • Production Possibility Frontier (PPF) — the curve of maximum output combos an economy can produce with fixed resources.

Microeconomics

📗 Market Equilibrium & Elasticity

  • Demand & Supply — equilibrium price/quantity is where the two curves intersect.
  • Price Elasticity — measures how responsive quantity is to a price change (%ΔQ ÷ %ΔP).
  • Surplus — consumer surplus (benefit above price paid) and producer surplus (revenue above cost).

Macroeconomics

📙 National Income & Inflation

  • GDP/GNP — total value of goods & services produced domestically vs. by nationals.
  • Inflation & CPI — general rise in prices, tracked via the Consumer Price Index.
  • Unemployment — share of the labor force without work but actively seeking it.

Agricultural Marketing

📕 Marketing Margins & Channels

  • Marketing Margin — the gap between farm gate and retail price, covering cost & profit of middlemen.
  • Marketing Channels — the path (farmer → wholesaler → retailer → consumer) goods travel through.
  • Value Addition — processing raw produce to raise its price and marketability.
